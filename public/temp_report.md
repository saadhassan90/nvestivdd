- **Team:** 49-person organization with 16+ year history of collaboration among core investment team
- **Ownership:** 100% woman-owned, NYC WBE-certified

1. **Experienced Management:** Founder Marjorie Hogan has 30+ years of structured credit expertise; trading team averages 30+ years experience with 10+ years working together
2. **Proprietary Infrastructure:** Extensive proprietary models (OAS pricing, market value coverage, default/recovery forecasts), automated screening dashboards, and 50+ tab CLO database
3. **Institutional Grade Track Record:** Consistent performance (1 down year in 15) with low correlation to major indices (0.04 correlation to Bloomberg US Aggregate Bond)
4. **Diversified Portfolio:** Current portfolio includes 200+ distinct loan issuers, 1,800+ positions, with no single CLO exceeding 3.9% of AUM
5. **Risk Management:** Dedicated Risk Officer (40+ years experience; 10+ years at Altum); formal daily/weekly/monthly risk governance; comprehensive hedging program
6. **WBE Certification:** Meaningful diversity credential in institutional asset management

### Moderate Concerns
1. **Concentration in CLO Strategy:** Portfolio is 100% CLO-focused with limited diversification outside structured credit
2. **Illiquidity Risk (Equity):** While CLO mezzanine notes are liquid, CLO equity positions can be difficult to liquidate in adverse markets
3. **Macroeconomic Sensitivity:** 2024 default rates elevated (7.1% peak in September); fund's ability to exploit "distressed" opportunities depends on continued market dislocation
4. **Information Asymmetry:** Extensive proprietary models and "hidden value" identification strategies rely on team expertise; potential challenges in transferability if key personnel depart
5. **Fund Scale:** Appears to be an emerging manager by asset levels; limited public disclosure of current AUM
6. **Market Timing Risk:** Strategy depends on ability to identify secondary market inefficiencies; crowded CLO market may reduce alpha generation

### Red Flags Identified
1. **Limited Regulatory Filings:** No SEC Form ADV or public regulatory documentation provided; registration status not confirmed
2. **Key Person Risk:** Founder Marjorie Hogan is heavily featured in all materials; succession planning not addressed
3. **Fund Documentation Gaps:** Missing performance attribution analysis; limited detail on side-pockets, redemption history, or gate events
4. **Valuation Risk:** Heavy reliance on proprietary marks; month-end pricing challenges; market value coverage dependent on daily pricing service availability
5. **Regulatory Uncertainty:** CLO secondary market trading faces ongoing regulatory scrutiny; leverage restrictions and documentation requirements may evolve

### Summary Assessment
Altum Capital presents as a **high-conviction, experienced specialist manager** with institutional-grade infrastructure and a strong 15-year track record. However, the firm should be classified as an **emerging to mid-size manager** with concentration risk, key person dependencies, and limited public regulatory transparency. The investment opportunity is suitable for **sophisticated institutional investors with high risk tolerance and a 3+ year investment horizon** who understand CLO market dynamics and accept liquidity constraints on equity positions.
