## 5. STRATEGY & MARKET VALIDATION (MODULE C)

### Investment Strategy Overview

**Fund Positioning:** Global opportunistic macro hedge fund integrating discretionary macro framework with bottom-up security selection across multiple asset classes (equities, credit, rates, FX, commodities).

**Strategy Philosophy (5 Key Pillars):**

1. **Macro-Micro Integration:** "We do not believe macro and micro are two distinct processes." Top-down macro orientation guides micro work; micro research validates macro thesis.

2. **Three Key Metrics Framework:**
   - **Macro Metric:** Is the macro environment a headwind or tailwind for this investment?
   - **Technical/Regulatory Metric:** Is political/regulatory backdrop a headwind or tailwind?
   - **Differentiated View Metric:** Do we have a unique micro/technical edge?

3. **Portfolio Concentration & Liquidity:** Concentrated portfolio (not disclosed how many positions, but 13F filings show 59-62 holdings) with premium on liquidity to allow rapid repositioning as regime shifts.

4. **P&L Cushion Strategy:** Capital preservation is paramount; risk management allows aggressive positioning on high-conviction views while maintaining "P&L cushion" (ability to sustain drawdowns without forced liquidation).

5. **Cross-Disciplinary Synthesis:** Research draws on global data analysis, country-specific analysis, reaction functions, historical analogs, technical/market analysis, and cross-disciplinary insights.
