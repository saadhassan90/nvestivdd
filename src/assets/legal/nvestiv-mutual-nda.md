# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

**This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into and made effective as of the Effective Date set out in the execution blocks below (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between:

**Party A — Sender / General Partner ("GP"):**
`{{GP_COMPANY_NAME}}`, `{{GP_ENTITY_TYPE_AND_JURISDICTION}}`, with its principal place of business at `{{GP_REGISTERED_ADDRESS}}`; and

**Party B — Recipient / Limited Partner ("LP"):**
`{{LP_COMPANY_NAME}}`, `{{LP_ENTITY_TYPE_AND_JURISDICTION}}`, with its principal place of business at `{{LP_REGISTERED_ADDRESS}}`.

Each of the GP and the LP is referred to individually as a "**Party**" and collectively as the "**Parties**." This Agreement is **mutual and bi-directional**: each Party may act as a **Disclosing Party** when it discloses Confidential Information and as a **Receiving Party** when it receives Confidential Information, and the obligations and protections in this Agreement apply to each Party in both capacities.

## Recitals

**WHEREAS**, the Parties have been introduced through, and intend to interact by means of, the Nvestiv platform (the "**Platform**", as further defined below), an operating system coordinating interactions between general partners and limited partners in the private alternative investment ecosystem;

**WHEREAS**, in connection with exploring, evaluating, negotiating, and potentially consummating an investment relationship between them, each Party may disclose to the other certain confidential, proprietary, or non-public information, whether through the Platform (including any data room made available through it), or through any other channel of communication occurring before, during, or after access to the Platform;

**WHEREAS**, the Parties wish to protect such information against unauthorized use or disclosure and to define their respective rights and obligations with respect to it;

**WHEREAS**, the operator of the Platform ("**Nvestiv**") facilitates the execution of this Agreement and the exchange of information between the Parties but is **not itself a Party** to this Agreement, except as an express non-party beneficiary of the Platform-related provisions set out in Section 6 and Section 19;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises set out in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows.

## 1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set out below. Defined terms used but not defined in a given Section have the meaning given to them elsewhere in this Agreement.

1.1. "**Affiliate**" means, with respect to a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party, where "control" means the ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests or the power to direct the management and policies of the entity.

1.2. "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 3.

1.3. "**Data Room**" means any secure digital repository of materials made available by or on behalf of a Disclosing Party to a Receiving Party through the Platform.

1.4. "**Disclosing Party**" means, at any given time, the Party (together with its Affiliates and Representatives) that discloses Confidential Information under this Agreement.

1.5. "**Material Non-Public Information**" or "**MNPI**" means any information relating to a Party, an investment fund, a portfolio company, a security, or an issuer that is not generally available to the public and that a reasonable investor would consider important in deciding whether to buy, hold, or sell any security, or that could reasonably be expected to affect the price of any security if made public.

1.6. "**Personal Data**" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, as such term (or any equivalent term, including "personal information") is defined under any applicable Privacy Law.

1.7. "**Platform**" means the Nvestiv operating system, applications, interfaces, and supporting systems through which the Parties interact, including any Data Room, messaging, document exchange, and electronic-execution functionality made available by Nvestiv.

1.8. "**Privacy Law**" means any applicable law, regulation, or binding regulatory guidance relating to the protection of Personal Data, including without limitation the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation ("**GDPR**"), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended ("**CCPA/CPRA**"), the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act of Canada ("**PIPEDA**"), and any analogous law in any applicable jurisdiction.

1.9. "**Purpose**" means evaluating, pursuing, negotiating, structuring, and potentially consummating a possible investment, co-investment, allocation, subscription, or related commercial relationship between the Parties, including all activities reasonably leading toward the closing of such a relationship.

1.10. "**Receiving Party**" means, at any given time, the Party (together with its Affiliates and Representatives) that receives or has access to Confidential Information under this Agreement.

1.11. "**Representatives**" means, with respect to a Party, its and its Affiliates' directors, officers, employees, partners, members, managers, agents, advisors (including legal, financial, tax, and accounting advisors), consultants, auditors, and investment committee members, in each case who receive Confidential Information in connection with the Purpose.

1.12. "**Residual Knowledge**" means ideas, concepts, know-how, and techniques that are retained in the unaided memory of a Receiving Party's Representatives who have had authorized access to Confidential Information, without intentional memorization and without reference to the Confidential Information in tangible form.

## 2. Purpose

2.1. Each Party may disclose Confidential Information to the other solely for the Purpose. Each Receiving Party agrees to use the Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party **only** for the Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever.

2.2. Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to disclose any particular information, to proceed with any transaction, or to enter into any further agreement. Any such obligation arises only under a separate definitive written agreement executed by the Parties.

---

## 3. Scope of Confidential Information

3.1. "**Confidential Information**" means any and all non-public, confidential, or proprietary information disclosed by or on behalf of a Disclosing Party to a Receiving Party in connection with the Purpose, **regardless of the channel, medium, or form of disclosure**, and regardless of whether disclosed before, during, or after access to the Platform, including without limitation information disclosed:

- (a) through the Platform, including any Data Room;
- (b) by email, messaging, file transfer, or other electronic means;
- (c) orally, including in meetings, calls, and presentations;
- (d) in writing or visually; and
- (e) by or to any Representative of a Party.

3.2. Without limiting the foregoing, Confidential Information includes: fund performance data, returns, and track records; portfolio company information; investment strategies, methodologies, and models; financial statements, projections, and valuations; fund terms, fee arrangements, and side-letter terms; an LP's investment mandate, assets under management, allocation targets, and internal analyses; deal flow and pipeline information; personnel information; legal, compliance, and regulatory information and correspondence; the existence, status, and contents of the discussions between the Parties; and any other information that is marked or identified as confidential or that, given its nature or the circumstances of disclosure, a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

3.3. Confidential Information includes all notes, analyses, compilations, studies, summaries, extracts, and other materials prepared by a Receiving Party or its Representatives that contain, reflect, or are derived from the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

3.4. Oral and unmarked disclosures that meet the standard in Section 3.1 and Section 3.2 are protected as Confidential Information whether or not subsequently identified or confirmed in writing.

---

## 4. Exclusions from Confidential Information

4.1. Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by competent written evidence:

- (a) was or became generally available to the public other than as a result of a breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives;
- (b) was lawfully in the Receiving Party's possession on a non-confidential basis prior to its disclosure by the Disclosing Party;
- (c) was independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or
- (d) was or is lawfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party not known to be under any obligation of confidentiality with respect to it.

4.2. A combination of features or information does not fall within an exclusion merely because individual features or items are within an exclusion; the combination itself must qualify.

---

## 5. Obligations of the Receiving Party

5.1. Each Receiving Party shall:

- (a) use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
- (b) hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence and protect it using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar importance, and in no event less than a reasonable degree of care;
- (c) not disclose the Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party, except as expressly permitted under this Agreement;
- (d) limit access to the Confidential Information to those of its Representatives who have a need to know it for the Purpose;
- (e) ensure that each such Representative is bound by confidentiality and use obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement, whether by written agreement or by professional or fiduciary duty, prior to receiving any Confidential Information; and
- (f) remain responsible and liable for any act or omission of its Representatives that would constitute a breach of this Agreement if committed by the Receiving Party.

5.2. The Receiving Party shall not, and shall not permit any of its Representatives to: (a) copy, reproduce, or extract Confidential Information except to the extent reasonably necessary for the Purpose; or (b) reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or otherwise attempt to derive any proprietary methodology, model, formula, or source of the Disclosing Party from the Confidential Information.

5.3. Nothing in this Agreement restricts a Receiving Party's Representatives from using Residual Knowledge for any purpose, provided that this Section 5.3 does not constitute a license to any patent, copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property right of the Disclosing Party, and does not authorize the disclosure of Confidential Information or the use of MNPI in violation of Section 8.

## 6. Channels of Disclosure; Platform and Data Room Provisions

6.1. The Parties acknowledge that Confidential Information may be exchanged through the Platform, including through one or more Data Rooms, as well as through other channels described in Section 3.1.

6.2. With respect to information made available through the Platform, the Receiving Party shall not, without the prior authorization of the Disclosing Party: (a) download, export, print, screenshot, or otherwise reproduce materials beyond what is reasonably necessary for the Purpose; or (b) circumvent, disable, or attempt to circumvent any access control, watermark, or security feature of the Platform.

6.3. The Receiving Party shall not attempt to circumvent the Platform in order to obtain Confidential Information directly or to evade the restrictions of this Agreement.

6.4. The Disclosing Party (or Nvestiv acting on its instruction) may suspend, restrict, or revoke the Receiving Party's access to any Data Room or Platform materials at any time and for any reason, without liability to the Receiving Party.

6.5. The Parties acknowledge that Nvestiv maintains access logs, audit trails, and execution records relating to the Platform, and agree that such records may be relied upon and admitted as evidence of access, disclosure, execution, or breach in any proceeding under this Agreement. Nvestiv is an express non-party beneficiary of this Section 6 and may enforce it to the extent necessary to protect the integrity of the Platform.

## 7. Compelled Disclosure

7.1. If a Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or by a court, regulator, or other governmental or self-regulatory authority of competent jurisdiction (including any securities regulator) to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permitted:

- (a) promptly notify the Disclosing Party in writing before disclosure, so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy;
- (b) reasonably cooperate with the Disclosing Party, at the Disclosing Party's expense, in any effort to obtain such protection; and
- (c) disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally required to disclose and use reasonable efforts to obtain assurance that confidential treatment will be afforded to the disclosed information.

7.2. The remainder of the Confidential Information not so required to be disclosed continues to be subject to this Agreement.

## 8. Securities Law Compliance; Material Non-Public Information

8.1. Each Receiving Party acknowledges that Confidential Information may constitute or contain MNPI, and that applicable securities laws may impose restrictions on the use and disclosure of MNPI, including the United States Securities Act of 1933 and Securities Exchange Act of 1934, the EU Market Abuse Regulation, the rules of the United Kingdom Financial Conduct Authority, the rules of applicable Canadian securities regulators, and analogous laws in any applicable jurisdiction.

8.2. Each Receiving Party agrees that it shall not, and shall not permit its Representatives to: (a) purchase, sell, or otherwise trade in any security while in possession of MNPI received under this Agreement, in violation of applicable law; or (b) communicate, "tip," or otherwise pass MNPI to any person for the purpose of, or where it is reasonably foreseeable that such person may, trade on the basis of such MNPI.

8.3. Each Receiving Party shall maintain such internal information barriers and compliance procedures as are reasonably necessary to comply with this Section 8 and applicable securities laws.

8.4. The obligations in this Section 8 are in addition to, and not in limitation of, any obligation imposed by applicable law, and survive in accordance with Section 14.3.

## 9. Privacy and Data Protection

9.1. To the extent a Party processes Personal Data made available by the other Party under this Agreement, each Party shall comply with all applicable Privacy Laws.

9.2. Each Party acts as an **independent controller** (and not as a joint controller or processor of the other) with respect to the Personal Data it receives, and is independently responsible for determining the purposes and means of its own processing and for its own compliance with applicable Privacy Law.

9.3. A Receiving Party shall: (a) process Personal Data only as necessary for the Purpose; (b) apply the principles of data minimization, purpose limitation, and storage limitation; and (c) not further disclose Personal Data except as permitted under this Agreement and applicable Privacy Law.

9.4. Where Personal Data is transferred across borders, the Parties shall implement an appropriate transfer mechanism required by applicable Privacy Law, including standard contractual clauses, an adequacy decision, or another lawful safeguard.

9.5. Each Party shall reasonably cooperate with the other to enable the other to respond to requests from data subjects and from competent supervisory authorities relating to Personal Data processed under this Agreement.

## 10. Data Security

10.1. Each Receiving Party shall implement and maintain commercially reasonable technical and organizational security measures designed to protect Confidential Information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction, appropriate to the nature and sensitivity of the information.

10.2. Each Receiving Party shall restrict access to Confidential Information to authorized Representatives and shall use secure means for the storage and transmission of Confidential Information.

10.3. A Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party of any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access to, or disclosure or loss of, the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information **as soon as reasonably practicable** after becoming aware of it, and in any event without undue delay and no later than seventy-two (72) hours where required by applicable Privacy Law, and shall reasonably cooperate with the Disclosing Party to investigate and remediate the incident.

## 11. No Solicitation; No Hire

11.1. During the term of this Agreement and for a period of **twelve (12) months** following its expiration or termination, neither Party shall, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment or engagement, or hire or engage, any key employee or partner of the other Party with whom it had contact or about whom it learned in connection with the Purpose.

11.2. The restriction in Section 11.1 does not prohibit: (a) general solicitations or advertisements not specifically targeted at the other Party's personnel; or (b) the hiring of any person who responds to such a general solicitation or who independently approaches the hiring Party without solicitation in breach of this Section 11.

Nothing in this Agreement grants, by implication, estoppel, or otherwise, any license or any right, title, or interest in or to any Confidential Information or any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual property right of the Disclosing Party.

12.2. The Disclosing Party makes **no representation or warranty**, express or implied, as to the accuracy or completeness of any Confidential Information. Neither Party, nor any of its Representatives, has any liability to the other resulting from the use of, or reliance upon, the Confidential Information, except as expressly set out in this Agreement. Each Receiving Party is solely responsible for its own evaluation and investment decisions.

12.3. Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to proceed with any transaction or relationship.

## 13. Return or Destruction of Confidential Information

13.1. Upon the written request of the Disclosing Party, upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, or upon the abandonment of the Purpose, the Receiving Party shall, at the Disclosing Party's election, promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in its possession or control, together with all copies, and shall, upon request, certify such destruction in writing.

13.2. The Receiving Party is not required to return or destroy Confidential Information to the extent: (a) it is required to retain it by applicable law, regulation, or bona fide internal compliance or document-retention policy; or (b) it resides in routine, automated electronic backup systems not readily accessible, provided that any retained Confidential Information remains subject to the confidentiality and use obligations of this Agreement for so long as it is retained, notwithstanding the survival period in Section 14.

## 14. Term and Survival

14.1. **Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues in effect for the duration of the Parties' discussions and dealings in connection with the Purpose, unless earlier terminated by either Party upon written notice to the other.

14.2. **Survival — General.** The confidentiality and use obligations of this Agreement with respect to Confidential Information survive the expiration or termination of this Agreement for a period of **one (1) year** following such expiration or termination.

14.3. **Survival — Excepted Categories.** Notwithstanding Section 14.2, the obligations of the Receiving Party with respect to: (a) **MNPI** (Section 8); (b) any Confidential Information that constitutes a **trade secret** under applicable law; and (c) **Personal Data** (Section 9), shall continue for so long as the information remains MNPI, a trade secret, or protected Personal Data, respectively, or for so long as required by applicable law — in each case beyond the one (1) year period in Section 14.2.

14.4. Termination of this Agreement does not relieve either Party of any liability arising from a breach occurring prior to termination.

## 15. Indemnification

15.1. Each Party (the "**Indemnifying Party**") shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other Party and its Affiliates and Representatives from and against any losses, liabilities, damages, costs, and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) to the extent arising out of or resulting from the Indemnifying Party's or its Representatives' breach of this Agreement.

15.2. The obligations in this Section 15 survive the expiration or termination of this Agreement.

## 16. Remedies; Equitable Relief

16.1. Each Party acknowledges that a breach or threatened breach of this Agreement may cause the other Party irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy.

16.2. Accordingly, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity, the non-breaching Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance, and other equitable relief to prevent or restrain a breach, **without the necessity of posting a bond or other security** and without the necessity of proving actual damages.

16.3. The rights and remedies under this Agreement are **cumulative** and in addition to, and not in lieu of, any other rights or remedies available at law or in equity.

16.4. Notwithstanding the agreement to arbitrate in Section 18, either Party may apply to any court of competent jurisdiction for interim or conservatory measures, including injunctive relief, in aid of the Purpose of this Agreement, and such application is not a waiver of the agreement to arbitrate.

## 17. Limitation of Liability

17.1. Subject to Section 17.2, neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, exemplary, or punitive damages, or for lost profits, in each case arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort, or otherwise, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

17.2. The limitation in Section 17.1 does **not** apply to, and there is **no cap** on, liability arising from: (a) a breach of the confidentiality or use obligations in this Agreement; (b) a breach of Section 8 (MNPI); (c) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 15; or (d) a Party's fraud, gross negligence, or willful misconduct.

## 18. Governing Law; Dispute Resolution

18.1. **Worldwide Application.** This Agreement and the obligations of the Parties apply worldwide, regardless of the location from which Confidential Information is disclosed, accessed, or used.

18.2. **Governing Law.** This Agreement, and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims), is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the jurisdiction designated by the GP in **Schedule B** (the "**Designated Jurisdiction**"), without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. If no jurisdiction is designated in Schedule B, the Designated Jurisdiction is the jurisdiction of the GP's principal place of business as stated in this Agreement.

18.3. **Arbitration.** Subject to Section 16.4, any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement, including its existence, validity, interpretation, performance, breach, or termination, shall be finally resolved by arbitration administered under the **Rules of Arbitration of the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC)**, or such other internationally recognized arbitral institution and rules as the GP may designate in Schedule B (and, if none is designated, the ICC).

18.4. The **seat (legal place) of arbitration** shall be the Designated Jurisdiction. The arbitration shall be conducted by one (1) arbitrator (or three (3) where the amount in dispute or the complexity reasonably warrants, as provided by the applicable rules). The **language** of the arbitration shall be English unless the Parties agree otherwise. The arbitration, and all materials and awards in it, shall be **confidential**.

18.5. The Parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the Designated Jurisdiction for any application permitted under Section 16.4 and for the recognition and enforcement of any arbitral award, and each Party waives any objection to such courts on the grounds of venue or forum non conveniens. An arbitral award rendered under this Section 18 is final and binding and may be enforced in any court of competent jurisdiction.

## 20. General Provisions

20.1. **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous understandings, agreements, representations, and communications, whether written or oral, relating to that subject matter.

20.2. **Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument (including an electronically executed instrument) agreed to by both Parties.

20.3. **Waiver.** No waiver of any provision of this Agreement is effective unless made in writing and signed by the waiving Party. No failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of that right.

20.4. **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, that provision is severed or limited to the minimum extent necessary, and the remaining provisions continue in full force and effect.

20.5. **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that a Party may assign this Agreement to an Affiliate or in connection with a merger, reorganization, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets, in each case to a successor that agrees in writing to be bound by this Agreement.

20.6. **No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for Nvestiv's rights as an express non-party beneficiary under Sections 6, 18, and 19, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns, and confers no rights on any other person.

20.7. **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and may be given through the Platform's notification functionality or to the email address of the relevant Party's signatory stated in the execution blocks below, and are deemed given upon confirmation of delivery or transmission.

20.8. **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts (including electronic counterparts), each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one and the same instrument.

20.9. **Independent Parties.** The Parties are independent contracting parties. Nothing in this Agreement creates any partnership, joint venture, agency, or fiduciary relationship between them.

20.10. **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance (other than the obligation to maintain confidentiality and to refrain from prohibited use or disclosure) caused by events beyond its reasonable control.

20.11. **Headings.** Headings are for convenience only and do not affect the interpretation of this Agreement.
